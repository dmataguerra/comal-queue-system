\subsection{Business context}

The restaurant's ticketing and sales processes remain outside Comal++. The operator calls an order when it is ready; customers read the TV and hear the announcement. The normal installation uses a single PC and an extended display connected to it.

Historical meeting notes describe an 08:30-15:00 operating day. This is context, not a schedule enforced by the application. Actual staffing, delivery procedures, noise levels and hardware must be confirmed by the restaurant.
