\section{Glossary}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.70\textwidth}}
\toprule
Term & Repository-grounded meaning \\
\midrule
COMAL Queue & Canonical documentation name for the repository. The UI also uses Troyanos and historical text uses Comal++. \\
Turn / Turno & One stored ready-order record with an internal UUID and visible two-digit ticket number. \\
Ticket number & Existing code copied by staff, normalized by the UI to \Code{01}--\Code{99}; it is not the database identity. \\
Ready / Listo & The only active turn status. A newly created turn enters this state immediately. \\
Delivered / Entregado & Closed status selected after pickup; the turn remains in history but leaves the active snapshot. \\
Cancelled / Retirado & Closed status used for an erroneous capture; it remains in history and leaves the display. \\
Recall / Reannounce & Explicitly raise a ready turn's rank, update its last-announced timestamp, and emit a new announcement. \\
Counter / Mostrador & Pickup location encoded as 0 (none), 1, or 2. \\
Cashier workspace & Operational interface rendered on all SPA routes except those beginning with \Code{/pantalla}. \\
Public display & Read-only screen at \Code{/pantalla}; it renders the queue, media, footer, and visual announcement overlay. \\
Announcement & Ephemeral Socket.IO payload causing a visual callout and, when enabled, sequential turn/counter audio. It is not stored as its own table. \\
Snapshot & Current ready turns plus multimedia, playlists, and settings emitted to clients. \\
Rank & Monotonically increasing integer defining newest/recalled-first ordering across turn history. \\
Request ID & Client-generated idempotency key stored with a fingerprint and serialized response. \\
Fallback media & The non-YouTube visual carousel shown when YouTube is inactive, unavailable, blocked, or not selected. \\
Playlist & Server-discovered local directory of supported audio files, or a YouTube playlist identifier when media type is YouTube. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
