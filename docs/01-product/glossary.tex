\subsection{Product glossary}

Ticket: external order number entered by the operator. Visible number: integer 0-99, formatted as two digits. Current: most recently called visible number. Called list: up to five earlier visible numbers. Announcement: session-local ID and number sent for visual/audio playback.

Correction: one-level silent restoration of the previous call state. Removal: explicit deletion from the visible list, without business delivery semantics. Expiry: removal five minutes after the number's latest call. Delivery acknowledgement: software playback status, not proof that speakers produced sound.

Evidence: nucleo/turnos.ts; shared/contract.ts
