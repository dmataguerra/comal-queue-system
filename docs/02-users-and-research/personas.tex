\subsection{Working personas and research limits}

Operator persona: needs fast keyboard calling, visible confirmation and a short, actionable error when saving or audio capacity fails. Customer persona: needs legible two-digit numbers and audible announcements in the restaurant environment. Support persona: needs diagnostic routes, logs and a reversible recovery procedure.

These personas are derived from source and operational notes, not formal interviews or measured usability research. No conversion rate, satisfaction result or accessibility certification is asserted.
