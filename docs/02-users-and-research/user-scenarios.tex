\subsection{User scenarios}

Normal call: enter 213298 and press Enter; after successful persistence, 98 becomes current and is announced. Repeat: enter 98 again; its announcement receives a new ID and its five-minute validity restarts. Correction: restore the previous call state silently once.

Saturation: six active/pending announcements prevent another call before mutation; keep the input and retry when capacity returns. Audio failure: the persisted number stays visible, while the operator checks the failed acknowledgement and repeats after fixing the cause.

Offline: local queue, voices, videos and banners continue; desktop YouTube falls back to local content. Browser YouTube is explicitly unavailable.

Evidence: backend/queue-store.ts; main/entregas-audio.ts; vistas/publica/Contenido.tsx
