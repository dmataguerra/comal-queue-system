\section{Authentication and authorization}

\Status{NOT_IMPLEMENTED}.

There is no login page, user table, password, hash library, session, token, cookie, identity provider, Nest guard, role decorator, route middleware, or protected frontend route. The public display is read-only only by presentation; it shares network access to unauthenticated mutation endpoints.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.48\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
Capability & Operator UI & Public UI & API enforcement \\
\midrule
View active state & Yes & Yes & None \\
View up to 200 historical turns & No & No & Public endpoint \\
Create/recall/update turns & Yes & No & None \\
Configure media/settings & Yes & No & None \\
Refresh media catalog & Yes & No & None \\
Read catalogued media & Indirect & Yes & None \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

An authorization policy cannot be invented from current code. Stakeholders must determine whether trusted loopback/physical access is sufficient or whether a LAN deployment requires operator authentication and/or network segmentation. Until then, no document should describe Cashier or Administrator as enforced roles.
