\subsection{Audio and multimedia architecture}

Desktop source priority: configured and available YouTube, playable local videos, banners, then welcome branding. Browser skips YouTube and uses local fallback. Local videos use a shuffled bag, avoid immediate repeat when possible and skip failures. Banners default to eight seconds.

The announcement FIFO allows six tasks including active, a 45-second start deadline and 30-second execution limit. Local Web Audio plays notice and selected voice with one or two repetitions, after multimedia ducking is confirmed. Missing, muted, undecodable or interrupted voice does not confirm success.

Audio inventory prefers nonempty MP3, otherwise WAV; 00-99 and notice are required. Byte size/timestamps affect revisionAudio, invalidating decoded buffers on replacement. YouTube starts muted, confirms volume before playback and reports health from player state; failure triggers local fallback.
