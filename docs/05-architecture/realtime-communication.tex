\subsection{Realtime communication}

The application uses Socket.IO with WebSocket-only frontend transport. It does not use polling, SSE, Redis, or a message broker.

\begin{longtable}{P{0.18\textwidth}P{0.19\textwidth}P{0.19\textwidth}P{0.34\textwidth}}
\toprule
Event & Producer & Consumer & Payload/purpose \\
\midrule
\Code{state} & EventsGateway & all frontend views & Full \Code{AppState}: active turns, multimedia, catalog, settings. Emitted on connection and after every successful mutation/refresh. \\
\Code{announcement} & EventsGateway & live frontend views & Announcement ID, turn ID, number, counter, ISO creation time. Emitted only after non-replayed create/recall. \\
\Code{connect} & Socket.IO client runtime & SystemProvider & Marks the client connected; state normally follows from gateway connection handling. \\
\Code{disconnect} / \Code{connect_error} & Socket.IO client runtime & SystemProvider & Marks disconnected; announcement queue is cleared by the public hook. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{Connection lifecycle}

The client enables reconnection with delay starting at 800 ms and capped at 5 seconds. No custom connection authentication, namespace, room, heartbeat setting, maximum reconnect count, or application acknowledgement is defined. Socket.IO defaults apply where the repository is silent.

\subsubsection*{Ordering and failure behavior}

Full-state broadcasts reduce incremental reconciliation complexity. Announcement delivery is best effort: it is not persisted as an event stream, acknowledged, or replayed. The public hook serializes received announcements, discards duplicate IDs in a bounded in-memory set, skips events whose turn has closed, and aborts on disconnect. Multiple server processes would not share gateway broadcasts.
