\subsection{Product roadmap}

This roadmap contains no dates or commitments. Items derive from gaps between documented ready-order intent and current behavior.

\begin{longtable}{P{0.10\textwidth}P{0.25\textwidth}P{0.55\textwidth}}
\toprule
Priority & Capability & Evidence-based outcome \\
\midrule
P0 & Audible public calls & Provide an explicit, tested audio-activation journey per public screen; verify number/suffix sequencing and media duck/resume offline. \\
P0 & Safe operating model & Decide whether displays are mirrored or network clients; approve security and manual-continuity model for that topology. \\
P0 & Recoverable operation & Approve and exercise backup/restore and power/service failure procedures. \\
P1 & Operational acceptance & Validate turn creation, recall, delivery, cancellation, restart, and disconnect workflows on target hardware and screens. \\
P1 & Display completeness & Add/approve an empty-queue message and validate page rotation/viewing distance/readability. \\
P1 & Administrative boundary & Decide whether media/settings require a distinct administrator role or remain cashier functions. \\
P1 & History policy & Define who may view history, required fields, retention, search/export, and privacy treatment. \\
P2 & Operator efficiency & Validate need for search/filter, scanner/keyboard flow, error correction, and long-queue handling before implementing them. \\
P2 & Media policy & Define allowed content, Internet/privacy expectations, per-screen sync need, and local library ownership. \\
P3 & Brand/visual evolution & Select canonical COMAL/Comal++/Troyanos identity and decide fate of unused mascot/reference assets. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Preparation tracking, POS integration, payments, inventory, invoicing, and ticket printing remain explicitly outside the prior documented scope; they are not roadmap items without a new stakeholder decision.
