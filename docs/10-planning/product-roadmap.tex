\subsection{Product roadmap}

Recommended acceptance priorities are target-hardware legibility/audibility, clear operator recovery guidance and validation of the six-number/six-audio limits at real peak load. These are recommendations, not promised release dates or stakeholder-approved scope.

If multiple terminals, remote displays, historical order tracking or account roles are requested, define requirements and trust boundaries before extending the current local product. No such capability is implied by the optional browser transport.
