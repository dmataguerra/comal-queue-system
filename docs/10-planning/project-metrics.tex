\subsection{Metrics and interpretation}

Operational evidence includes call/repeat/correction/expiry counts, software audio acknowledgements, dispatch latencies, process CPU/memory, log sizes and endurance duration. The endurance report records environment, limitations and RUNNING/PASS/FAIL or PREFLIGHT\_PASS status.

Dispatch latency is not time until audible sound. Queue occupancy summaries are action-based, not time-weighted. Temperature probing is optional. No benchmark, sales metric, throughput SLO, usability score or production uptime is inferred from these reports.
