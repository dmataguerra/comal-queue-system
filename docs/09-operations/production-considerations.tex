\subsection{Production acceptance}

Validate a release candidate on the intended standard Windows account with TV in extended mode and the correct sound output. Exercise disconnect/reconnect, offline content, corruption/recovery in isolated data and the exact installer upgrade/rollback.

The endurance script runs 390 minutes (6 h 30 min), matching the historical 08:30-15:00 span. PREFLIGHT\_PASS is not full PASS. Logs, metrics and report must identify the artifact/environment; physical audibility still needs human acceptance.

Configure production environment reviewers and tag restrictions in GitHub, approved signing secrets/thumbprint and resource distribution rights. Declaring an environment in YAML does not prove protections are enabled.
